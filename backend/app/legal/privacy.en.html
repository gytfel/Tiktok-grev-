This Policy explains what data $app (the “Service”) processes, why, and how you can control it. The Service is operated by $owner, $country (“we”, “us”). Contact: $email.

1. Data we process

Team members who sign in to the Service

  • username and role;
  • password, stored only as a secure hash;
  • two-factor authentication secret, stored encrypted;
  • time zone, notification preferences and, if you turn on notifications, your browser’s push subscription;
  • a log of actions performed in the Service, for example who confirmed a publication and when.

TikTok accounts connected by their owners

When an account owner signs in with TikTok and grants permissions, TikTok provides us with:

  • basic profile information: open ID, display name, username and avatar;
  • access and refresh tokens, stored encrypted, and the list of granted permissions;
  • publishing settings of the account, such as the available privacy options, the maximum video length and whether comments, Duets and Stitches are allowed;
  • for videos published through the Service: the post ID and the publication status.

If statistics are turned on, we also receive the account’s follower, following, likes and video counts, and the view, like, comment and share counts of videos published through the Service.

Content

Videos uploaded to the Service, captions, comment reply drafts, templates and reminders created by team members.

Technical data

Server logs with IP addresses and request times, kept to operate and protect the Service.

2. How we use the data

We use the data only to:

  • publish the videos you choose to the accounts you connected, with the settings you confirmed;
  • show publication status and statistics in the Service;
  • send the notifications you turned on;
  • keep the Service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising or profiling, and do not use TikTok data to train AI models.

3. Who we share data with

  • TikTok: videos, captions and post settings are sent to TikTok through its official API to publish them.
  • Hosting provider: the Service runs on a rented server, where the data is stored.
  • Push services (Apple, Google, Microsoft, Mozilla): they deliver notifications to your devices. The content of notifications is encrypted.
  • AI provider, only if the administrator turns on the optional AI assistant: the text you enter in the assistant, for example a topic or a draft caption, is sent to the provider to generate suggestions. TikTok tokens and account data are never sent.

We may also disclose data when required by law.

4. Storage and security

Data is transferred over HTTPS. TikTok tokens and two-factor secrets are encrypted in the database and passwords are hashed. $signin

5. Retention

  • TikTok tokens are kept while the account is connected. When the account is disconnected, the Service revokes the token at TikTok and deletes it.
  • When an account is deleted from the Service, its profile data, publication history and statistics are deleted too.
  • Uploaded videos are kept until a team member deletes them.
  • Deleted data may remain in database backups for up to $backup_days days. Copies of videos in backups are removed on request.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can disconnect a TikTok account at any time in the Service or in the TikTok settings for connected apps. After that the Service can no longer access the account.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by writing to $email. We reply within 30 days.
  • You can turn notifications on or off on the Notifications page.
